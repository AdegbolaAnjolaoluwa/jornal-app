Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Your entries are never made public, never shared with other users, and never sold. This policy explains exactly what we collect, which outside services are involved in making the app work, and what "private" does and doesn't mean here.

1. What we collect

Account information

  • Email address and password (stored as a secure hash, never in plain text)
  • Name and nickname, if you provide them
  • Timezone
  • Age, if you choose to provide it during sign-up — optional and used only for personalization, not verified
  • Profile picture, if you upload one

Journal content

  • Entry text, whether typed or transcribed from a voice recording
  • Voice recordings themselves, if you use voice entries
  • AI-generated reflections, action points, clarifying questions, tags, and summaries based on your entries

Usage information

  • Basic analytics about how you use the app (see Section 3)
  • IP address and timing information used for rate-limiting sign-in, sign-up, and password-reset attempts

2. How we store your data

Your account information and journal content, including voice recordings, are stored in our database. We want to be direct about this: entry text and audio are not encrypted beyond the standard encryption already provided by our infrastructure providers (such as encryption of data in transit, and whatever encryption-at-rest our hosting and database providers apply at the infrastructure level). There is no additional, journal-specific encryption layer on top of that. Access to your data is restricted to your own account through the application's access controls.

3. Who else processes your data

We use a small number of outside services to make Say So work. Here's exactly what each one sees:

Service What it's used for What it receives
Groq (AI processing) Generating reflections, action points, clarifying questions, tags, recap summaries, and answers to your questions about your own journal The text of your entries and, where relevant, previously extracted facts and entry excerpts
PostHog (analytics) Understanding how the app is used, to help us improve it Your email address, an internal user ID, and app usage events — not your journal content

We don't use these services to sell your data, and we don't share your journal content with any other third party, advertiser, or other user of the app.

4. Cookies and sessions

We use a single session cookie to keep you signed in. It's set to be inaccessible to page scripts and is only sent over a secure connection. If you choose "remember me" when signing in, this cookie lasts up to 30 days; otherwise it expires when you close your browser or after 24 hours, whichever comes first. We don't use third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

5. Data retention

We keep your account and journal content for as long as your account is active. You can delete individual entries yourself at any time. See Section 7 for full account deletion.

6. Exporting your data

You can request a full export of your entries, action points, and tags as a JSON file at any time from within the app, so your journal is never locked in.

7. Deleting your account

Full account deletion isn't yet a self-service feature in the app. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at anjeesax@gmail.com and we'll complete the request within 30 days.

8. Children's privacy

Say So is not intended for use by anyone under the minimum age described in our Terms of Service. We don't knowingly collect data from children below that age.

9. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us at anjeesax@gmail.com.
