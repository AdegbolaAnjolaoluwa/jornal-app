Frequently asked questions

Last updated: August 3, 2026

What is Say So?

Say So is a place to capture what's on your mind by voice or text. After you write or record an entry, it reflects what you said back to you, pulls out any action points, and asks a clarifying question if something seems worth following up on.

Is my journal private?

Your entries are never made public and never shared with other users — there's no social or sharing feature anywhere in the app, and your data is never sold. To generate reflections, action points, and summaries, your entry text is sent to Groq, the AI provider that powers those features (see our Privacy Policy for the full list of who processes your data and why). "Private" means it stays yours and off-limits to other users — not that no service ever touches it in order to work.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Say So can generate a full export of your entries, action points, and tags as a JSON file, so your journal is never locked in.

Can I delete my account?

Account deletion isn't yet a self-service button in the app. To request full deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at anjeesax@gmail.com and we'll process the request within 30 days. You can delete individual entries yourself at any time from within the app.

What happens to voice entries?

When you record a voice entry, the audio is transcribed and stored alongside the entry so you can play it back later. Like text entries, the transcript is used to generate your reflection and action points.

What are tags, Recap, and Ask?

Tags let you label entries with your own freeform categories, with autocomplete so you naturally reuse the same ones. Recap is a weekly or monthly AI-generated summary of what you've been writing about, including your most-used tags. Ask lets you ask a natural-language question about your own journal history — like "when did I last mention Sam?" — and get an answer grounded only in what you've actually written; if nothing relevant is found, it says so rather than guessing.

Why am I sometimes asked to slow down?

Sign-in, sign-up, and password reset are all rate-limited to protect accounts from abuse. If you hit a limit, wait a few minutes and try again.

Yes — see our Terms of Service for the age requirement.
